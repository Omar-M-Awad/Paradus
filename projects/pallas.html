Full Concept Rebrand

Pallas

Create what time cannot diminish.

Buildova was a company without a philosophy — no shared idea holding its identity, direction or presence together. Pallas is a full rebranding concept built to fix exactly that.

The concept
A new name, a new logo, a new philosophy, a new direction and a new color system — a complete identity built from the ground up.
Philosophy
“Create what time cannot diminish.” A line for a company that had never defined what it stood for.
Status
An independent concept, not a live engagement. The business owner liked it a lot — especially the identity, philosophy and direction it gave the company.
