OffScript

Beyond the Script.

OffScript is an independent game studio built by a team of friends with one shared goal: to make games without being confined to a single genre, formula or path.

Philosophy
“We believe games should never be limited by expectations. We explore, experiment, and create without being confined to a single path. Every project is a chance to go further.”
Personality
Bold · Curious · Experimental · Human · Ambitious
Voice
Speaks like a group of creators, not a corporation — confident without pretending to be bigger than it is, playful without being childish, ambitious without being arrogant, and honest about the journey of building games.
Role
Co-Founder & CEO. The brand work was strong enough that the founder brought Omar in as a co-founder.
