App Rebrand

Circle

Circle is a peer-to-peer rental marketplace. It connects people who need something temporarily with owners whose items sit unused — so renters get access without buying, and owners turn idle belongings into extra income.

The rebrand
A complete rebrand: new logo, new website, new direction and a new color system, giving the product a confident, recognisable identity.
